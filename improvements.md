**Do not self-fund the Risk Analyst Certificate.** Its stated audience is "Junior Risk
Analysts, Middle Officers, Trader Assistants, IT Business Analysts and Auditors" — the
jobs being applied for. It is training employers give staff, not a credential that gets
you hired, and the member rate applies because the trading houses themselves are the
members. The next intake (Mar–Jun 2027) also falls after this application cycle. Correct
play: name it in interview as training you want, and ask about it at offer stage.
<https://suissenegoce.ch/programs/commodity-trading-risk-analyst-certificate/>

**The CHF 1,300 Fundamentals course is the one worth paying for now.** Two days in Geneva,
capped at 20 participants, October or November 2026 — early enough to sit before/during the
autumn graduate cycle. The room full of Geneva industry people is likely worth more than the
syllabus, and it converts "self-study" on the CV into a dated Education line.
<https://suissenegoce.ch/programs/commodity-trading-fundamentals/>

- **FRM Part I** remains the better fit if targeting market/credit risk specifically.
  <https://www.garp.org/frm>
